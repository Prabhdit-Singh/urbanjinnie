Game Info

How to play

  • Pick a Target Multiplier (or set a Win Chance % — they're linked) and place your bet.
  • A provably-fair result multiplier is generated. If it's ≥ your target, you win bet × target. Otherwise you lose the bet.
  • Win Chance = 99% ÷ target. Lower targets win more often for a smaller payout; higher targets pay big but hit rarely — same expected return either way.
  • Autoplay can adjust your bet after each win/loss by a percentage, and stop automatically once a profit or loss threshold is reached.

Bonus Buy

  • Rush Mode (accessible) — buys 10 rapid-fire shots at your current Target Multiplier. It's exactly 10 independent normal bets fired back to back; cost = 10× bet, same RTP as a normal bet.
  • Triple Shot (premium) — one draw, three escalating gates at your chosen starting target × 1 / 10 / 100. Clearing a gate banks its own prize on top of any earlier one — the highest gate you clear, the more you've already banked underneath it. Your starting target (2×–900×) is the risk dial: pick it higher for a bigger, rarer payout. Cost is always 3× bet, and — because the gate values cancel out mathematically — RTP is exactly 99% for every possible starting target.
  • Jackpot Shot (extreme) — buys one single draw with a disclosed prize range (MIN WIN 25× – MAX WIN 100,000×). Below the minimum it's a miss; at or above it, the draw's own value (capped at the maximum) becomes the payout — no separate prize table or wheel, just one provably-fair number.
  • All three reuse the exact same HMAC-SHA256 draw as a normal bet — see the fairness panel to verify any bonus round the same way you'd verify a normal one.

Math

House edge 1% → target RTP 99% at every target multiplier (verified by tools/simulate.js across thousands of random targets). Result multiplier is floored to 2 decimal places and floored at 1.00× (an instant win at the lowest possible target); the maximum target/result is 1,000,000×.
