Game Info

How to play

  • 6×5 grid, pays anywhere: card royals pay with 9+ matching symbols anywhere; creatures are premium and pay with just 3+.
  • Column Creature Battle: every creature lands with a multiplier. When 2+ creatures land in the same column they battle — the highest multiplier wins the column. Equal top multipliers combine (x4 vs x4 → x8). 3+ creatures means a mini tournament with the same rules.
  • Total spin multiplier: all winning column multipliers are added together and applied to the total symbol win — after wins are calculated.
  • Golden Egg (Wild): counts toward every card-royal win, and adds +x2 to any battle in its column.
  • Goldhorn: the legendary beast rolls x10–x100, and its presence doubles the column winner.
  • Free Spins: 3, 4, 5+ Battle Arena Tickets award 10, 12, 15 free spins. Battles happen more often, multipliers roll hotter, and winning battle multipliers carry over for the whole bonus. 3+ battles in one spin: +2 extra spins; 3+ tickets: +5 spins.
  • Bonus Buy: buy free spins for 100× bet.
  • Max win: 10,000× bet. The round ends when the cap is reached.
