Game Info

How to play

  • 5×3 reels, 10 fixed lines: line wins pay left-to-right from reel 1, 3 or more matching symbols.
  • The Book is Scatter & Wild: it substitutes for every symbol and pays anywhere on the screen — 2, 3, 4 or 5 Books pay 1×, 2×, 20× or 200× total bet.
  • Free Spins: land 3+ Books to win 10 Free Spins. One symbol is chosen at random as the Special Expanding Symbol.
  • Expanding Symbol: during Free Spins the special symbol expands to cover its whole reel and pays on any position. 3+ Books retrigger another 10 spins.
  • Max win: 5,000× total bet.
