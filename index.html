GAME INFO & PAYTABLE

How to play

  • 6×5 grid, pay anywhere: land 8 or more matching symbols anywhere on the grid to win — position doesn't matter.
  • Germ Explosion (tumble): winning symbols explode, the rest fall and new symbols drop in. Tumbles continue while new wins form.
  • Wild Med Kit: substitutes for every paying symbol.
  • Invasion Meter: Germ Blob explosions charge the meter. A full meter fires the Scanner Beam — random symbols turn Wild.
  • Lab Portal (Scatter): 3+ trigger Emergency Lab Spins (10/12/15/20). Portals also pay 3–100×.
  • Serum Multiplier Orbs: in free spins, orbs are collected into a total multiplier (up to ×100) that multiplies every win.
  • Bonus Buy: Free Spins 100× · Scanner 250× · Outbreak 500× · Virus King 1000×.
  • Max win: 5000× bet — the round ends when the cap is reached.
