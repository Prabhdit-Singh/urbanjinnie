How to play

  • 7×7 grid, cluster pays: land 5 or more matching symbols connected horizontally or vertically.
  • Tumble: winning symbols pop, the rest fall down and new candies drop in. Tumbles continue while new wins form.
  • Multiplier spots: every win leaves a mark on its cells. A second win on a marked cell turns it into a ×2 spot; each further win doubles it, up to ×1024. A cluster's win is multiplied by the sum of spot values beneath it.
  • Free Spins: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 Crystal Cubes award 10, 12, 14, 16, 18 free spins. Marks and multiplier spots persist for the entire bonus. 3+ scatters during the bonus award +10 extra spins.

  • Bonus Buy / Super Bonus Buy: buy free spins for 100× bet, or super free spins (pre-placed multipliers, spots start at ×2) for 500× bet.
